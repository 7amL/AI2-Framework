Executive Summary 
 
The AI² Framework provides a structured, risk-informed approach to managing the 
unique risks introduced by Artificial Intelligence.  
 
 
 
Developed to support both strategic and technical stakeholders, the framework fulfills 
three core objectives: 
 
Enable AI Adoption Through Risk Mitigation 
 
The AI² Framework helps organizations establish compliant, research-driven AI 
risk strategies to safely adopt and benefit from AI. Built on insights from nearly 
thirty publications, it bridges broad industry guidance with targeted 
implementation steps.  
 
Establish the Adversary Imitation Assessment Function 
 
AI² introduces a novel adversarial testing methodology - adversary imitation - as 
an extension of the organization’s offensive security function. This pivot occurs 
after the Risk Management Framework (RMF) step and is key to aligning controls 
with AI-specific threats.
