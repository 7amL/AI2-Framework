# AI² Framework

The AI² Framework is a structured, risk-informed methodology for adversarial testing and risk profiling of AI systems. It integrates industry-aligned practices from NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, and Microsoft’s red-teaming guidance to assess and mitigate the unique risks of generative and agentic AI.

---

## What It Covers

- Risk identification and categorization
- AI system decomposition via AI Solution Layers
- Mapping risks to adversarial tactics and MITRE ATLAS techniques
- Assessment methodology for offensive testing of AI
- Pre-assessment checklist and tooling
- Alignment to RMF controls and regulatory frameworks
